How it works

The five-stage journey

StageWhat happens
1. ApplicationYou fill in the application form with your skills and what you are looking for.
2. Screening callA short call to understand your working style and ambitions.
3. MatchingWe match you with complementary founders.
4. IntroductionYou receive an intro with a written "matching thesis".
5. SupportPost-intro support, events and a peer network.

FAQ

QuestionAnswer
Is it free?Yes, Foundry is free for founders.
